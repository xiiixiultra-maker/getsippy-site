1. What Sippy is

Sippy is a habit and accountability app. It schedules alarms and uses your device camera to confirm you took a sip of water.

Sippy is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. Hydration needs vary by person, activity, climate, medication, and medical history. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional about your own hydration — and never use Sippy in place of medical guidance.

2. Use it safely

  • Do not use Sippy while driving or doing anything that requires your attention.
  • Do not force fluid intake beyond what is comfortable. Drinking too much water too quickly can be dangerous. Sippy caps daily goals and warns on rapid logging, but you are responsible for listening to your body.
  • Sippy is for personal, non-commercial use by people 13 and older.
