7. Alarms

Sippy uses Apple's AlarmKit, so its alarms can ring through silent mode and Focus. When you press Stop, the alarm comes back:

  • Gentle: every 2 minutes, up to 2 times.
  • Firm (the default): every 90 seconds, up to 5 times.
  • Relentless: every 60 seconds, with no limit.

If an alarm is ignored or swiped away, backup alarms ring again a few minutes later (Gentle: after 3, 6 and 9 minutes; Firm: 2, 5 and 8; Relentless: 2, 4 and 6) until you answer it or it is silenced.

In Hard Mode it comes back with no limit, at whichever pace you chose. A camera sip check answers the alarm, and the free emergency bypass silences it. In the default (Bestie) mode, after 2 failed camera tries Sippy offers an honor button instead.

Alarms need an active Sippy Pro subscription or free trial, and they stop if your subscription ends. They also depend on iOS: they won't ring if your phone is off or if you turn off Sippy's permission to schedule alarms. Don't rely on Sippy for anything safety-critical or time-critical, such as medication, appointments or travel.
